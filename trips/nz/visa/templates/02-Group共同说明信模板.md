# Two-person Group Visitor Visa Cover Letter Template

> 使用方法：由 group administrator 完成，控制在 1–2 页，导出为 PDF。每名成员仍需提交自己的个人资料和财务、工作证明。

```text
[Date]

To: Immigration New Zealand
Subject: Group Visitor Visa Application – [GROUP NAME]

Dear Visa Officer,

We are applying as a group of two for a holiday in New Zealand from
[ARRIVAL DATE] to [DEPARTURE DATE]. We share the same purpose of travel and the
same travel arrangements, and we intend to travel to and arrive in New Zealand
together.

Group members
1. [FULL NAME], passport number [NUMBER], date of birth [DATE]
2. [FULL NAME], passport number [NUMBER], date of birth [DATE]

Group roles
- Group administrator: [NAME]
- Group contact: [NAME, MOBILE, EMAIL]
- Group leader in New Zealand: [NAME, MOBILE]

Travel plan
We will spend [NUMBER] days in New Zealand. Our planned route is
[CITY/REGION 1] – [CITY/REGION 2] – [CITY/REGION 3]. We will use the same
flights, accommodation and main itinerary. The detailed itinerary and booking
evidence are attached.

Funding
[Each member will pay their own expenses. / NAME will pay DETAILS for NAME.]
Each member has provided personal evidence of funds and employment or other
reasons to return home.

Declaration
We confirm that the information in this letter is true and that we intend to
travel to and arrive in New Zealand together for the same purpose and under the
same travel arrangements.

Signed:

_________________________       _________________________
[MEMBER 1 FULL NAME]             [MEMBER 2 FULL NAME]
[DATE]                           [DATE]
```
