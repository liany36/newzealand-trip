# Individual Visitor Visa Cover Letter Template

> 使用方法：替换所有方括号内容，删除不适用段落，控制在 1–2 页，导出为 PDF。两个人分别出具，事实和日期必须一致，但工作、资金和回国约束应按本人情况填写。

```text
[Date]

To: Immigration New Zealand
Subject: Visitor Visa Application – [FULL NAME, PASSPORT NUMBER]

Dear Visa Officer,

I am applying for a Visitor Visa to travel to New Zealand from [ARRIVAL DATE]
to [DEPARTURE DATE] for a holiday with my [partner / boyfriend / girlfriend /
travelling companion], [COMPANION FULL NAME].

Purpose and itinerary
We plan to spend [NUMBER] days in New Zealand. Our planned route is
[CITY/REGION 1] – [CITY/REGION 2] – [CITY/REGION 3]. A detailed itinerary,
accommodation plan and proposed flight details are attached.

Travel companions
My travelling companion is:
- Full name: [FULL NAME]
- Date of birth: [DD MONTH YYYY]
- Passport number: [PASSPORT NUMBER]
- Relationship to me: [RELATIONSHIP]
- Visa application number: [NUMBER, IF AVAILABLE]

Funding
[I will pay for my own flights, accommodation and daily expenses. / NAME will
pay for the following expenses: DETAILS.] I have attached my bank statements,
income evidence and [funding declaration, if applicable].

Employment and reasons to return
I am employed as [JOB TITLE] by [EMPLOYER] and have worked there since [DATE].
My leave has been approved from [DATE] to [DATE], and I am expected to return
to work on [DATE]. [Add other accurate commitments, if relevant.]

Travel compliance
I understand the conditions of a New Zealand Visitor Visa. I will not work in
New Zealand and will leave New Zealand by [DEPARTURE DATE].

Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully,
[FULL NAME]
[MOBILE]
[EMAIL]
[RESIDENTIAL ADDRESS]
```
