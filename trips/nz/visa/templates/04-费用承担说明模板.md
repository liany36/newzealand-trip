# Travel Funding Declaration Template

> 使用方法：只有一方替另一方支付部分或全部费用时使用。双方签字，附付款方银行流水及收入证明，导出为 PDF。

```text
[Date]

To: Immigration New Zealand
Subject: Travel Funding Declaration for [FUNDED PERSON FULL NAME]

I, [FUNDER FULL NAME], passport number [NUMBER], confirm that I will pay the
following costs for [FUNDED PERSON FULL NAME], passport number [NUMBER], during
our trip to New Zealand from [ARRIVAL DATE] to [DEPARTURE DATE]:

- [Return flights]
- [Accommodation]
- [Ground transport]
- [Daily living expenses]
- [Activities]

Our relationship is [ACCURATE RELATIONSHIP DESCRIPTION]. We have known each
other since [DATE] and [briefly describe the relationship and, if applicable,
the living arrangement].

I have attached my recent bank statements and evidence of income to show that I
can meet these costs without borrowing funds for the visa application.

Funder signature: __________________  Date: ______________
Name: [FULL NAME]
Mobile: [NUMBER]
Email: [EMAIL]

Funded person signature: ___________  Date: ______________
Name: [FULL NAME]
```
