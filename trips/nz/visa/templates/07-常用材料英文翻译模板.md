# 常用签证材料英文翻译模板

> 将方括号替换为原件内容。原件中没有内容的字段写 `N/A`；无法辨认的内容写 `[Illegible]`；印章和签名写成 `[Official stamp: ...]`、`[Signature]`。姓名使用护照拼音。

## 1. 中国居民身份证

```text
ENGLISH TRANSLATION OF PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA RESIDENT IDENTITY CARD

FRONT
Name: [PASSPORT ENGLISH NAME]
Sex: [Male / Female]
Ethnicity: [ETHNICITY]
Date of Birth: [DD Month YYYY]
Residential Address: [FULL ENGLISH ADDRESS]
Citizen Identity Number: [ID NUMBER]

REVERSE
Issuing Authority: [AUTHORITY]
Period of Validity: [DD Month YYYY] to [DD Month YYYY / Long-term]
```

## 2. 户口簿封面及户主页

```text
ENGLISH TRANSLATION OF HOUSEHOLD REGISTER

Document: Household Register of the People's Republic of China

Type of Household: [NON-AGRICULTURAL / AGRICULTURAL / OTHER]
Name of Head of Household: [NAME]
Household Number: [NUMBER]
Residential Address: [FULL ENGLISH ADDRESS]
Issuing Authority: [AUTHORITY]
Date of Issue: [DD Month YYYY]
[Official stamp: DESCRIPTION]
```

## 3. 户口簿个人页

```text
ENGLISH TRANSLATION OF HOUSEHOLD REGISTER – INDIVIDUAL PAGE

Name: [PASSPORT ENGLISH NAME]
Relationship to Head of Household: [RELATIONSHIP]
Former Name: [NAME / N/A]
Sex: [Male / Female]
Place of Birth: [PLACE]
Ethnicity: [ETHNICITY]
Place of Ancestral Origin: [PLACE]
Date of Birth: [DD Month YYYY]
Other Residential Address in This City/County: [ADDRESS / N/A]
Religion: [DETAIL / N/A]
Citizen Identity Number: [ID NUMBER]
Height: [NUMBER] cm
Blood Type: [TYPE / N/A]
Education: [LEVEL]
Marital Status: [STATUS SHOWN ON ORIGINAL]
Military Service Status: [STATUS / N/A]
Employer: [EMPLOYER / N/A]
Occupation: [OCCUPATION / N/A]
Date and Place of Moving into This City/County: [DETAILS / N/A]
Date and Place of Moving into This Address: [DETAILS / N/A]
Registration Officer: [NAME / Signature]
Date of Registration: [DD Month YYYY]
Changes and Corrections: [FULL DETAILS / N/A]
[Official stamp: DESCRIPTION]
```

> 户口簿各地版式不同。字段必须以自己的原件为准，原件多出的栏目也要补充翻译。

## 4. 银行流水

```text
ENGLISH TRANSLATION OF BANK STATEMENT

Bank Name: [BANK]
Branch: [BRANCH]
Account Holder: [PASSPORT ENGLISH NAME]
Account Number: [NUMBER]
Currency: [CNY / OTHER]
Statement Period: [START DATE] to [END DATE]
Opening Balance: [AMOUNT]
Closing Balance: [AMOUNT]

| Date | Time | Transaction Description | Counterparty | Debit | Credit | Balance | Channel/Remarks |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| [DATE] | [TIME] | [FULL TRANSLATION] | [NAME] | [AMOUNT] | — | [AMOUNT] | [DETAILS] |
| [DATE] | [TIME] | [FULL TRANSLATION] | [NAME] | — | [AMOUNT] | [AMOUNT] | [DETAILS] |

[Translate every transaction and every note on every submitted page.]
[Official stamp: DESCRIPTION, if present]
```

## 5. 中文在职及准假证明译文

```text
ENGLISH TRANSLATION OF EMPLOYMENT AND LEAVE CERTIFICATE

Date of Issue: [DD Month YYYY]

This is to certify that [PASSPORT ENGLISH NAME], passport number [NUMBER], has
been employed by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] as [JOB TITLE] since [START DATE].
[His/Her/Their] current [monthly/annual] salary is [AMOUNT AND CURRENCY].

The company has approved [his/her/their] leave from [DATE] to [DATE].
[He/She/They] is expected to resume work on [DATE].

Company Name: [FULL NAME]
Company Address: [ADDRESS]
Contact Person and Title: [DETAILS]
Telephone: [NUMBER]

[Signature: NAME/TITLE]
[Official company stamp: DESCRIPTION]
```

> 必须按中文原件逐项翻译。原件未出现的工资、返岗日期或其他内容不能自行添加。

## 6. 订单或其他文件

```text
ENGLISH TRANSLATION OF [DOCUMENT NAME]

Applicant/Guest/Passenger: [NAME]
Document or Booking Number: [NUMBER]
Issuer/Platform: [NAME]
Issue/Booking Date: [DATE]

[Translate every heading, field, paragraph, amount, date, status and remark in
the same order as the original document.]

[Official stamp/signature, if present: DESCRIPTION]
```

## 7. 翻译人员声明

将以下声明放在每套完整译文末尾：

```text
TRANSLATOR DETAILS AND DECLARATION

Applicant: [APPLICANT FULL NAME]
Document translated: [DOCUMENT NAME]
Source language: Chinese
Target language: English
Date of translation: [DATE]

I, [TRANSLATOR FULL NAME], confirm that I translated the attached document from
Chinese into English completely and accurately to the best of my knowledge and
ability. I am not the visa applicant, a family member of the applicant, or the
immigration adviser assisting with this application.

Translator's full name: [FULL NAME]
Address: [FULL ADDRESS]
Phone number: [PHONE]
Email: [EMAIL]
Qualifications and relevant Chinese-English translation experience: [DETAILS]

Signature: __________________________
Date: ______________________________
```
