# Employment and Leave Approval Letter Template

> 使用方法：由单位出具，使用单位抬头纸，写明签发日期，负责人签字并尽量加盖公章。联系方式应可供核实。导出为 PDF。

```text
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]

[Date]

To: Immigration New Zealand

RE: EMPLOYMENT AND LEAVE CONFIRMATION FOR [EMPLOYEE FULL NAME]

This letter confirms that [FULL NAME], passport number [PASSPORT NUMBER], has
been employed by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] as [JOB TITLE] since [START DATE].

[His/Her/Their] current [monthly/annual] salary is [AMOUNT AND CURRENCY].

The company has approved [his/her/their] paid leave from [LEAVE START DATE] to
[LEAVE END DATE] for a private holiday in New Zealand. [He/She/They] is expected
to resume work on [RETURN-TO-WORK DATE]. [His/Her/Their] position will remain
available during the approved leave.

Please contact us if further verification is required.

Yours sincerely,

[SIGNATURE]
[NAME OF AUTHORISED SIGNATORY]
[JOB TITLE]
[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]
[COMPANY ADDRESS]
[DIRECT PHONE]
[WORK EMAIL]
[COMPANY STAMP, IF USED]
```
